The logline

One guy wakes up.

In a backwards world where everyone is glued to the feed and money is the only score that counts, one ordinary guy wakes up. What he finds is funnier, stranger and kinder than anyone expected, and it might just wake up everybody else.

What it's about

Screens that never sleep, stuff stacked to the clouds and a planet that has had about enough. EARTH holds up a funhouse mirror to modern life, then points it toward something better.

How it feels

Big laughs, wild visuals and grown-up allegories, kept family-friendly with no cussing. Think silly on the surface, true underneath.

How it's made

AI-generated characters, voices and imagery, written and directed by people and built scene by scene like a studio shoot.
