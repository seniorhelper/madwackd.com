How we work

An AI-powered pipeline with human hands on the wheel

We move at internet speed without cutting the parts that matter: story, taste and craft.

  1. Story first

    Concepts, scripts and jokes are written and rewritten by people until they land.

    Human

  2. Design the world

    Characters, sets and looks are generated with AI image tools and directed frame by frame.

    AI + human direction

  3. Cast the voices

    AI character voices, plus real performers when a role calls for it, all directed for timing and feel.

    AI + human direction

  4. Build scene by scene

    Films are made in short scenes, like a studio shoot, so every beat gets reviewed.

    AI + human direction

  5. Cut, score, ship

    Editing, music, sound and final approval happen with human ears and eyes.

    Human

Straight talk: our characters, voices and imagery are often AI-generated, and we always say so. The writing, directing and final calls are human. Any resemblance to real persons is coincidental.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Madwackd Productions™?

Madwackd Productions™ is an AI-powered film and video production studio headquartered in Denver, Colorado, founded by producer Zach Wennstedt. We make comedy shorts, cartoons, parody, ads, brand films, music videos and documentaries, and our first feature film, The Earth MovieTHe EArtH mOVIe, is in production.

What kinds of projects does Madwackd produce?

Comedy shorts, animated cartoons for kids and adults, parody, commercials and brand films, music videos, documentaries and feature films. We produce our own originals and take on hired projects for businesses, brands and artists.

Does Madwackd use AI to make films?

Yes, and we say so openly. We use AI tools to create characters, voices and imagery, and every project is written, directed, edited and approved by people. AI gives us speed and wild visuals. Human taste decides what makes the cut.

What is The Earth MovieTHe EArtH mOVIe and when does it come out?

The Earth MovieTHe EArtH mOVIe is a family-friendly satirical feature about a backwards world addicted to its feeds, where one guy wakes up and learns that love and kindness matter more than money. It is in production and planned for Summer 2027 at watchvrmovie.com. You can join the waitlist at theearthmovie.com.

Can I hire Madwackd to produce an ad, short or music video?

Yes. Choose "Hire us to produce" in the form and tell us what you need, your timeline and a rough budget range. Every project is scoped and agreed in writing before work begins.

How can actors, voice talent and collaborators get involved?

Use the form and include a link to your public reel or portfolio. There is no promise of roles or pay, submissions are reviewed at our discretion, and we may not be able to reply to everyone.

Can I partner with Madwackd?

We welcome conversations with partners, studios and brands through the Work with us form. Nothing on this site is an offer to sell, or a solicitation of an offer to buy, any security.

Can I send you my script or story idea?

Please don't. We do not accept unsolicited scripts, treatments or story ideas, and unsolicited creative material is not reviewed and creates no obligation. It is standard studio practice and protects both sides. Links to public reels and portfolios are welcome.

Studios, brands and creators: the door is open and the coffee is on.
