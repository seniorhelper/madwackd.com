Who we want to meet

Six ways to get involved with an AI film production company

Madwackd Productions™ works with partners, actors, voice talent, collaborators, brands and press. Here is what each conversation looks like.

Partners and studios

Distribution, co-production, streaming and platform partners, sponsors, and brands that want to be part of a story people talk about.

Everything happens only by written agreement signed by both sides.

Actors and voice talent

On-camera performers and voice actors for live action, voice work and AI-assisted characters. Include a link to your public reel.

No promise of roles or pay. Submissions are reviewed at our discretion, and we may not reply to everyone.

Collaborators

Animators, editors, composers, musicians, sound designers and creators. Share links to public work you are proud of.

Collabs happen only by written agreement. Please do not send story ideas or scripts.

Brands and businesses

Hire us for AI commercial production, brand films, explainer shorts, social video and music videos. Tell us the goal, timeline and budget range.

Every project is scoped and agreed in writing first. No results are promised.

Press, podcasts and friends

Writing about AI filmmaking or indie film? Want to cheer us on? Say hello.

We read every note and reply when we can.
What happens next

After you hit send

  1. We read it

    Your note goes straight to the studio, not a sales list.

  2. We check the fit

    We look at your links and what you have in mind.

  3. We reply

    If it is a fit, we reach out by email to set up a call.

  4. We put it in writing

    Any project, role or partnership starts with a written agreement.

  5. We make it

    Then the fun part: building something nobody has seen.

Plain-English fine print

Investors: Nothing on this site is an offer to sell, or a solicitation of an offer to buy, any security.
